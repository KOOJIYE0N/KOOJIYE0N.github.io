MIM-VLA: Learning Physical Interaction Representations from Gripper Motor Feedback

A motor-feedback-enhanced VLA architecture that converts gripper current, position, velocity, and signal validity into an interaction token for contact-aware grasping and evidence-conditioned object selection.

arXiv Preprint Oct. 2026 · Under review

From Legs to Wheels: Embodiment-Aware Human Motion Retargeting for Mobile-Base Humanoids

An embodiment-aware motion-retargeting framework that allocates reconstructed human motion across a mobile base, vertical lift, and dual arms, with simulation replay and deployment on a physical robot.

Aerospace Science and Technology 2026 · Under revision

Depth-Fused Imitation Learning for Manipulator-Assisted Spacecraft Berthing

A depth-fused diffusion policy for two-phase spacecraft berthing, designed to remain robust when individual camera views are lost during terminal manipulation.

SSRN
ICRA 2026 / arXiv Jun. 1–5, 2026

RetoVLA: Reusing Register Tokens for Spatial Reasoning in Vision-Language-Action Models

RetoVLA repurposes register tokens from vision transformers as global spatial context for robot action generation. The method injects these tokens into the action expert, improving spatial reasoning while keeping the model lightweight.

Kalman-Conditioned Action Chunking Transformers for Dynamic Object Grasping Toward Aerial Recovery Systems

A dynamic grasping study that conditions Action Chunking Transformers with state estimation cues, aiming to improve robot-arm interaction with moving objects in aerial recovery scenarios.
